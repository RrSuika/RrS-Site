# 站点开场 / preloader — 完整规格

> 来源：原 `CLAUDE.md`「The site intro / preloader」整段（逐字搬运）。
> 涉及文件：`Layout.astro` 的 SITE INTRO GUARD（`<head>` 内联脚本 + 标记）、`src/scripts/site-intro.ts`、`global.css §16`。
> 参考原件：`输入/开场动画.html`——**当前参照是 2026-09-19 09:08 那一版**（用户当天替换过，更早版本的笔记已失效）。
> 改完必须实测的四条：F5 播放 / 站内跳转不播放 / reduced-motion 不播放 / guard 的 6s 兜底能把封面打开。探针必须用 `Page.reload`。

- **The site intro / preloader** (`Layout.astro` SITE INTRO GUARD + `src/scripts/site-intro.ts`, §16 of global.css): a gridded plate, a wordmark, one **striped rail along the bottom edge** lit from both ends toward the middle (`clip-path: inset()` driven by a `--reveal` custom property), a `[NNN]` counter with a status line, and three corner labels. ⚠️ **REBUILT 2026-09-19 from the SECOND version of `输入/开场动画.html`** ("我希望开场动画至少有快速是 0-100 的切换，另外参考我提供的新的开场动画.html"). What came over: the plate, the grid, the counter, the corner labels, the status line, the bottom rail and the exit. ⚠️ **The rail is TWO bars closing on the middle** (2026-09-19: "改成两条，分别从画面底部的左右侧往底部中间移动"): the left one grows rightward from the left end, the right one leftward from the right end, meeting at the centre at 100%. ⚠️ Both live layers are **FULL WIDTH** and clipped to their own half-window (`inset(0 calc(100% − var(--reveal)/2) 0 0)` and its mirror) — two half-width elements would put the 8/3 px stripe PHASE out of step at the join, because the centre lands mid-period on every viewport that is not a multiple of 11 px, and the half-windows cannot overlap so there is nothing to z-order. ⚠️ **The `[NNN]` counter carries NO glow** ("去掉进度条百分比的荧光发光特效"): it is the one thing on the plate being read, and a bloom softens the digit edges at exactly the size where legibility is already hardest. ⚠️ **The exit is a SIGNAL SWEEP, not a document parting**: a full-screen panel in `--accent` grows from the bottom edge (`scaleY(0→1)`, 620 ms, `cubic-bezier(.85,0,.15,1)`), holds 280 ms while the readout steps out in a stagger, then dissolves — `opacity → 0` over **1250 ms with `blur(12px)`**, so it scatters rather than retracts. ⚠️ That dissolve is deliberately the SLOWEST beat of the exit, lengthened on the user's call (2026-09-19: "橙色画面结束后，消失并且显示实际界面的这个消失过渡动画时间做长一点，让过渡柔和一些") — at 720 ms / `blur(7px)` it read as the panel being pulled off rather than as an arrival. ⚠️ It must stay in step with `SWEEP_FADE_MS` in `src/scripts/site-intro.ts`, which is what removes the node afterwards: a shorter CSS fade would leave a transparent full-screen cover over the live page. Measured on a real `Page.reload` (150 ms samples): sweep starts ~1.5 s, panel at full height ~2.1 s, `is-revealing` at ~2.4 s, then opacity 0.83 → 0.14 → 0.06 → 0.02 → 0 with the blur ramping 2.0 → 10.4 → 11.8 → 12.0 px, node removed ~3.4 s. The old two-curtain open is GONE. ⚠️ **What was deliberately NOT taken: the scanlines and the chromatic-aberration text shadow.** Those are the *retro-terminal* half of the reference's look, and this site's language is instrument panel — RGB fringing on the wordmark would be a costume change rather than a style match. (The current reference has no scanlines of its own either.) The signal colour is the live `--accent`, never the reference's hard-coded amber, so the rail, the counter and the sweep are legible in both themes and the intro agrees with the page it covers.
  - **The progress model is "fast 0 → 100, then a fast-forward if the page is early"** (`COUNT_MS` 2200). The counter takes the reference's own curve, `max(t, easeOutCubic(t) · 0.85)` — an eased head that is already at ~50% in the first quarter, handing over to a linear tail that lands on **exactly 100** (measured: 49% at 0.55 s, 74% at 1.1 s, 100% at 2.2 s). ⚠️ **It must REACH 100.** The revision this replaces climbed to 85% and then approached 100% asymptotically and never got there (99.95%), which is the honest answer to "接近百分百的时候稍微慢一点等加载" but means the number never shows 100 — and the last few percent of an asymptote are indistinguishable from a freeze anyway. ⚠️ When the page turns out to be ready early the remainder is **fast-forwarded**, not jumped: it still runs, over `SETTLE_MS` (260), landing no earlier than `MIN_MS` (1400) so a page ready on the first frame still gets a visible 0 → 100 and the full entrance choreography (measured on a warm cache: 56% at 0.57 s, 93% at 1.0 s, 100% at 1.43 s). ⚠️ If the count is spent and the page is NOT ready, the counter holds at 100 and the **status line** says so ("Waiting on assets", with the readout pulsing) — the wait is reported by the text, never by pretending the number still has somewhere to go. ⚠️ The status ladder (`Initialising` → `Loading assets` → `Calibrating layers`) is driven by the COUNT, not by the load: keyed on the load, a fast page goes straight from "Initialising" to "Ready" and the boot sequence is never legible.
  - ⚠️ **`OPTIONAL_MS` (2200) is separate from `MAX_MS` (3200)** and that is not a micro-optimisation: the black-hole video and the webfonts are nice to have and the text and layout are not. Waiting for all of them for the full cap means a slow video holds the cover for three seconds and then the status snaps from "waiting" straight to the end — the visitor waited for something that was never coming. The optional assets get their own earlier deadline so the intro can say "nearly there" and then genuinely finish. Measured worst case (a page that never fires `load`): 100% at 2.4 s, "Waiting on assets" until the cap, ready and sweeping at 3.4 s, cover gone at 5.2 s.
  - ⚠️ **The plate is dropped while the sweep is opaque, and that is what makes the dissolve land on the page.** `.site-intro` itself has NO background — `.site-intro__plate` carries it — so `.is-revealing` can hide the plate, the stage and the rail in the frame the panel is at full height (`SWEEP_GROW_MS + SWEEP_HOLD_MS` after the sweep starts). Without it the visitor watches the panel fade to the *paper* and then sees the paper cut away one frame later. Verified by pinning the panel at `scaleY(1)` and photographing it: 18 900 sampled points across both themes, **0 outside the panel colour family**, `rect = [0, 0, vw, vh]` at `z-index: 20`.
  - ⚠️ **Every entrance animation uses `animation-fill-mode: backwards`, NEVER `both`/`forwards`.** They animate `opacity`, and a filled final keyframe **outranks every normal declaration** — with `both` the staged fade-out (`.is-sweeping .site-intro__mark { opacity: 0 }` and friends) silently does nothing, because the animation keeps handing the element back `opacity: 1`. `backwards` holds the `from` state through the delay, plays, then returns the element to its base rule. The reference itself uses `forwards` here and its staged exit is inert for exactly this reason.
  - ⚠️ **No corner registration crosses** (removed 2026-09-19 on the user's call — four tiny crosses read as a print-shop joke at that size). ⚠️ **The cover takes the THEME**: `:root[data-theme="dark"] .site-intro` swaps paper/ink, so dark opens on `#0a0a11` and light on `#f1ece1`. A white sheet in both themes was the reference's own move and read as a flashbang here — the intro is the first frame of the page, so it has to be the first frame of the palette, which it can be because the boot script sets `data-theme` before the first paint. ⚠️ **The rail's two tick colours are `color-mix(signal n%, paper)` per theme, not an alpha**: one alpha cannot be a quiet tick on paper and a quiet tick on black.
  - ⚠️ **The decision is made by an inline script in `<head>`, not by the module**: a module runs after the body is parsed, which is late enough that a link navigation would paint the cover for a few hundred ms and then snatch it away. Cases: **first page of the session → show; F5/reload → show; a link inside the site → skip; back/forward, reduced motion → skip** (`sessionStorage["rrsuika-intro-seen"]` + `performance.getEntriesByType("navigation")[0].type` — this is a plain multi-page build with no client router, so nothing else can tell a first visit from a nav click). ⚠️ **That also decides how to probe it**: a CDP `Page.navigate` is a `navigate`, so the cover is skipped and every measurement reads an empty document — drive the probe with a real **`Page.reload`** (`navigation.type === "reload"` plays it). ⚠️ **It must never be able to trap the site**: everything resolves against caps, the guard's own 6 s safety net opens the cover if the module never runs, `<noscript>` hides it with JS off, and the skip path removes the markup on `DOMContentLoaded` so a hidden full-page div does not ride along on every page. The guard owns `window.__rrsIntroGate` / `__rrsIntroResolve` so the shelf can hold its own curtain and rise on it — the two intros then play in sequence instead of the shelf's playing unseen underneath the paper.
